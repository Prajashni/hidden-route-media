Privacy Policy — Hidden Route Uploader

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who uses this app

Hidden Route Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Hidden Route. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

  • Upload videos (YouTube Data API) — to upload the channel's own videos and schedule them.
  • View the channel (YouTube Data API, read-only) — to read the channel's own video list.
  • View analytics (YouTube Analytics API, read-only) — to read the channel's own views, watch time and subscriber counts.

How data is stored

The sign-in token and all statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from any other person's Google account is accessed.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops the app from reading or uploading anything.
